Our Advanced Computing Courses

BCA

The BCA is an AICTE-approved IT undergraduate course focused on computer applications, software development, and core IT skills. It provides structured academic training aligned with industry requirements and emerging technology domains. This computer applications degree builds strong foundations in programming languages such as C, Java, and Python. Students also gain knowledge in databases, networking, web technologies, and operating systems. The program is designed as a practical alternative to B.Tech, emphasising applied computing knowledge. Bhawanipur Global Campus formerly known as NSHM Knowledge Campus, Kolkata, offers this structured IT degree in Kolkata with academic and industry alignment.

  • Problem Analysis
  • Design & Analysis of Algorithms
  • Artificial Intelligence
  • Web Technologies
  • Conduct Investigations of Complex Computing Problems
4 Years Duration | Full Time Mode Of Study

B.Sc. - Data Science

The Bachelor of Science in Data Science is a multidisciplinary undergraduate programme aligned with NEP 2020 and Outcome-Based Education principles. This BSc Data Science program overview integrates mathematics, statistics, computer science, and artificial intelligence for structured data-centric learning. The curriculum promotes experiential learning, project-based assessment, internships, and interdisciplinary electives. Ethical, legal, and societal dimensions of data usage are embedded within the academic framework. Bhawanipur Global Campus formerly known as NSHM Knowledge Campus, Kolkata, delivers this structured BSc Data Science program with research orientation and industry relevance.

  • Disciplinary Knowledge
  • Problem Analysis
  • Design & Development of Solutions
  • Ethics & Social Responsibility
  • Individual & Team Work
4 Years Duration | Full Time Mode Of Study

BCA - AI & Machine Learning

The BCA in Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning is an AICTE-approved IT undergraduate course focused on intelligent systems and predictive modelling. This programme builds strong foundations in programming, statistical mathematics, and artificial neural networks. The curriculum emphasizes development of data-driven algorithms to solve real-world problems. It serves as a technical alternative to B.Tech AI & ML with a practical application focus. This structured programme supports progression toward advanced roles aligned with a modern AI data science course pathway. Bhawanipur Global Campus formerly known as NSHM Knowledge Campus, Kolkata, delivers this industry-oriented computer programming degree.

  • Computational & Statistical Knowledge
  • Problem Analysis & Data Modelling
  • Design/Development of Intelligent Solutions
  • Conduct Investigations of Complex AI Problems
4 Years Duration | Full Time Mode Of Study
